## Getting Started

### Creating Your Account
1. Go to the FitFuel homepage
2. Click **"Sign Up"**
3. Enter your email and create a strong password
4. Enter your health information:
   - Age
   - Weight (kg)
   - Height (cm)
   - Gender
   - Activity Level (Sedentary to Extremely Active)
   - Fitness Goal (Weight Loss, Muscle Gain, Maintenance)
5. Click **"Create Account"**

Your daily calorie goal will be automatically calculated based on your information.

### Logging In
1. Click **"Login"** on the homepage
2. Enter your email and password
3. Click **"Sign In"**

Your session will remain active for 24 hours.

## Logging Meals

### Quick Log from Dashboard
1. Click on any meal type (Breakfast, Lunch, etc.)
2. Enter meal name and calories
3. Click **"Add"**

### Detailed Log from Meals Page
1. Go to **"Meals"** tab
2. Click **"Log New Meal"** button
3. Fill in the form:
   - **Meal Name**: What you ate (e.g., "Grilled Chicken Salad")
   - **Calories**: Total calories (required)
   - **Protein**: Grams of protein (optional, for macro tracking)
   - **Carbs**: Grams of carbs (optional)
   - **Fat**: Grams of fat (optional)
   - **Meal Type**: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, or Snack
   - **Date & Time**: When you ate (auto-fills today)
4. Click **"Log Meal"**

### Editing Meals
1. Go to **"Meals"** tab
2. Find the meal in your log
3. Click the **"Edit"** button
4. Modify values and click **"Save"**

### Deleting Meals
1. Go to **"Meals"** tab
2. Find the meal in your log
3. Click **"Delete"** button
4. Confirm deletion

### Using the Nutrition Database
1. Go to **"Nutrition"** tab
2. Browse food items or use the **Search** box
3. Filter by category (Vegetables, Fruits, etc.)
4. Click on a food to see detailed nutrition
5. Click **"Quick Add"** to add to your meal log

## Tracking Workouts

### Logging a Workout
1. Go to **"Workouts"** tab
2. Click **"Log New Workout"**
3. Fill in the form:
   - **Activity Type**: Choose from 14+ exercises (Walking, Running, Cycling, etc.)
   - **Duration**: How many minutes (required)
   - **Your Weight**: Used to calculate calories (default: 70kg)
   - **Date & Time**: When you worked out
   - **Intensity**: Light, Normal, High, or Maximum
   - **Notes**: How you felt, achievements, etc. (optional)
4. **Estimated Calories**: Automatically calculated using MET formula
5. Click **"Log Workout"**

### Supported Activities

| Activity | MET | Best For |
|----------|-----|----------|
| Walking | 3.5 | Warm-up, recovery |
| Jogging | 7.0 | Cardio, low-impact |
| Running | 9.8 | High-intensity cardio |
| Cycling | 7.5 | Lower body, cardio |
| Swimming | 8.0 | Full-body cardio |
| Gym | 6.0 | Strength training |
| Yoga | 3.3 | Flexibility, core |
| HIIT | 10.0 | Maximum intensity |
| Soccer | 10.0 | Team sport, cardio |
| Basketball | 8.0 | Team sport, cardio |
| Tennis | 8.0 | Agility, cardio |
| Jumping Rope | 12.3 | High-intensity cardio |
| Elliptical | 6.0 | Low-impact cardio |
| Rowing | 7.0 | Full-body cardio |

### Calorie Burn Calculation
Calories = MET × Weight (kg) × Duration (hours) × Intensity Modifier

**Example:**
- Activity: Running (9.8 MET)
- Duration: 30 minutes (0.5 hours)
- Weight: 70 kg
- Intensity: Normal (1.0)
- **Calories Burned** = 9.8 × 70 × 0.5 × 1.0 = **343 kcal**

- Total calories and duration summary

### Workout History
- Filter by activity type
- View last 7, 14, 30 days, or all time
- See trends in your exercise habits

## Settings & Preferences

### Profile Settings
1. Go to **"Settings"**
2. Click **"Personal Info"** section
3. Update:
   - Full Name
   - Email
   - Username (read-only)
4. Click **"Save Changes"**

### Health Profile
1. Go to **"Settings"**
2. Update health information:
   - **Age**: Your current age
   - **Weight**: Current weight (kg)
   - **Height**: Your height (cm)
   - **Gender**: Male/Female
   - **Activity Level**: 
     - Sedentary (little or no exercise)
     - Lightly Active (1-3 days/week)
     - Moderately Active (3-5 days/week)
     - Very Active (6-7 days/week)
     - Extremely Active (physical job or training)
   - **Fitness Goal**: Weight Loss, Muscle Gain, or Maintenance
   - **Daily Calorie Goal**: Set custom target
3. Click **"Save Profile"**

Your calorie goal is automatically recalculated based on these values.

### Notification Preferences
1. Go to **"Settings"** → **"Notifications"**
2. Toggle on/off:
   - **Daily Reminders**: Reminder to log meals
   - **Goal Alerts**: When you're close to goal
   - **Meal Suggestions**: Personalized recommendations
   - **Weekly Reports**: Summary of your week
3. Changes save automatically

### Appearance Settings
1. Go to **"Settings"** → **"Appearance"**
2. **Dark Mode**: Toggle between light/dark theme
3. **Text Size**: 
   - Small (90%)
   - Normal (100%)
   - Large (110%)
4. Changes apply immediately across all pages

### Privacy & Security
1. Go to **"Settings"** → **"Privacy"**
2. Configure:
   - **Profile Visibility**: Public or Private
   - **Progress Sharing**: Allow friends to see progress
   - **Analytics**: Help improve app by sharing anonymous data
3. Click **"Save Preferences"**

### Data Management
1. Go to **"Settings"** → **"Data"**
2. **Export Data**: Download all your data as CSV
3. **Clear All Data**: Remove all meals and workouts
   - ⚠️ This action cannot be undone
4. **Delete Account**: Permanently delete your account
   - Requires password confirmation
   - ⚠️ All data will be permanently deleted

## Tips & Tricks

### Pro Tips for Success

#### 🎯 Meal Logging Tips
- **Log meals immediately** after eating for accuracy
- **Use the nutrition database** for common foods
- **Save favorites**: Most recent foods appear at top
- **Portion sizes matter**: Use kitchen scale for accuracy
- **Include snacks**: Even small snacks add up

#### 🏋️ Workout Tips
- **Log the actual time you exercised** for accurate stats
- **Adjust intensity** based on how hard you worked
- **Add notes** about your performance for motivation
- **Variety is key**: Mix cardio and strength training
- **Rest days are important**: Don't feel guilty for rest

#### 📊 Tracking Tips
- **Check progress regularly** but not obsessively
- **Focus on weekly trends**, not daily fluctuations
- **Weight can vary 2-3 kg daily** due to water/food
- **Patience is key**: Real results take 4-6 weeks
- **Celebrate non-scale victories**: How you feel, energy, strength

#### 💡 Goal Setting Tips
- **Start with realistic goals**: 0.5-1 kg per week loss
- **Set process goals, not just outcome goals**
  - Example: "Log meals 6 days/week" vs. "Lose 10 kg"
- **Review goals monthly** and adjust as needed
- **Build habits gradually**: Change one thing at a time

## FAQ

### Q: What is a "goal"?
**A:** Your daily calorie goal is calculated based on your BMR (Basal Metabolic Rate) and activity level. It's the number of calories you should consume to achieve your fitness objective.

### Q: How is my daily calorie goal calculated?
**A:** 
1. **BMR** = Harris-Benedict formula based on age, weight, height, gender
2. **TDEE** = BMR × Activity Level multiplier
3. **Goal** = TDEE ± adjustment based on fitness goal
   - Weight Loss: TDEE - 500 (0.5 kg/week)
   - Muscle Gain: TDEE + 500 (0.5 kg/week)
   - Maintenance: TDEE

### Q: Can I eat zero calories one day and more the next?
**A:** Not ideally. The app tracks daily goals, but aim to hit goals consistently. Weekly average matters more than individual days.

### Q: Do I need to log water intake?
**A:** Water is displayed in your stats but isn't counted in calories (since it has zero). Log it for reference and habit tracking.

### Q: How accurate are calorie estimates for workouts?
**A:** The MET formula is a scientific estimate. Actual calories vary based on:
- Fitness level
- Metabolism
- Effort level
- Environmental factors

Use estimates as a guide, not exact science.

### Q: Can I edit workouts from the past?
**A:** Go to the workout history, find it, and click edit. Changes update your stats immediately.

### Q: What does "streak" mean?
**A:** Consecutive days where you logged meals AND hit your daily calorie goal (within ±5%).

### Q: Is my data private?
**A:** Yes! Your data is encrypted and never shared without permission. See Privacy Policy for details.

### Q: Can I export my data?
**A:** Yes! Go to Settings → Data → Export. Get a CSV file with all your meals and workouts.

### Q: What happens if I delete my account?
**A:** All data is permanently deleted after 30 days. You can recover within 30 days by logging in. After 30 days, it's gone forever.

### Q: How often should I weigh myself?
**A:** Once per week at the same time of day, under the same conditions. Daily weighting shows water weight fluctuations.

### Q: Can I set a custom calorie goal?
**A:** Yes! Go to Settings → Health Profile → Daily Calorie Goal. You can override the calculated value.

### Q: My calories look wrong. Why?
**A:** Common reasons:
- **Portion size estimates**: Use food scale for accuracy
- **Food database errors**: Manually correct if needed
- **Double logging**: Check if meal was logged twice
- **Time zone issue**: Check app settings

### Q: How do I get recommendations?
**A:** 
- **Meals**: Go to Nutrition tab, use filters and search
- **Workouts**: Go to Workouts tab, see recommended list
- **Daily Plans**: Go to Progress → Plans section

### Q: Can multiple people use one account?
**A:** No. Create separate accounts. Each person gets personalized goals and recommendations.

### Q: What if I take a break from logging?
**A:** That's okay! Just jump back in whenever ready. Your streak resets, but historical data remains.

### Q: How can I get support?
**A:** 
- **Email**: support@fitfuel.app
- **FAQ**: In the app under Help
- **Community**: Join our Discord server
- **Feedback**: Send suggestions through Settings → Feedback
